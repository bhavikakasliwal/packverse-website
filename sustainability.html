Our sustainability approach

Paper-based formats

Paper bags, envelopes, cartons and tubes can provide practical alternatives for suitable applications.

Material efficiency

Right-sizing and selecting an appropriate structure can help avoid unnecessary material use while protecting the product.

Responsible design

We encourage packaging decisions that balance protection, presentation, performance and end-of-life considerations.

IMPORTANT

Sustainability should match the application.

No single material is right for every product. Shelf life, moisture, strength, hygiene, transport and product protection all matter.

For each requirement, we can explore suitable material and format options rather than treating sustainability as a one-size-fits-all promise.
